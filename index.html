🔄 AI Construction Assistant Workflow

How the system processes a project from registration to action.

1
Project Registration
Project and sanctioned amount are recorded.
2
Location Verification
Registered and detected locations are compared.
3
Progress Monitoring
Actual progress is compared with expected progress.
4
AI Risk Detection
Delay, progress gap and cost variation are analyzed.
5
Construction Photo Verification
Site evidence is compared with the expected stage.
6
Problem Reporting
Field officers can submit observations.
7
AI Cost Estimation
A preliminary repair estimate is generated.
8
Recommended Action
The system suggests the next verification step.

⚠️ Prototype note: AI-assisted outputs are intended for monitoring and decision support. Final physical verification should be performed by an authorized field officer or engineer.
