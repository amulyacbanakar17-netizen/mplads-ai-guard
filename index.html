🤖 AI Construction Monitoring Workflow

The AI assistant follows a structured workflow to collect project information, identify risks, verify field evidence and recommend actions.

1

📍 Collect Project Data

Collect project location, sanctioned amount, expenditure, physical progress and implementation data.

2

👤 User / Officer Entry

Field officers can enter project updates, construction day, problems and observations.

3

🧠 AI Risk Analysis

The system compares financial progress, physical progress, delays and cost variation to calculate project risk.

4

📷 Visual Verification

Construction photographs are submitted as field evidence and compared with the expected construction stage.

5

🚨 Detect Problem

AI identifies possible delays, abnormal expenditure, progress gaps and construction risks.

6

💰 Estimate Impact

The system provides an indicative estimate of the potential financial impact of a reported problem.

7

🎯 Recommend Action

AI generates a recommended next action such as field verification, document review or progress update.

8

✅ Human Verification

The final decision remains with an authorized officer or engineer before official action is taken.
